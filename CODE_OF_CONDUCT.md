# RepoSentinel Code of Conduct

## English

RepoSentinel is committed to a welcoming, respectful, constructive, and safe community. This Code of Conduct applies to the repository, Issues, Pull Requests, code reviews, Discussions, official project spaces, project events, and maintainer communication with contributors.

### Expected behavior

Participants are expected to communicate professionally, assume good faith while asking for clarification, criticize ideas and implementation rather than people, respect different perspectives, accept correction openly, protect private information, and keep discussions relevant to the technical or documentation problem being addressed.

When reporting a problem, provide only the minimum safe context. Use neutral language, redact personal and confidential information, and give maintainers a reasonable opportunity to understand and address the issue.

### Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- Doxxing, unwanted contact, intimidation, stalking, or publishing private conversations without consent.
- Sexualized conduct or content directed at another participant without clear consent.
- Deliberate disruption, impersonation, spam, or malicious manipulation of project processes.
- Publishing credentials, private keys, `.env` contents, proprietary source code, or unpatched vulnerability details in a public channel.
- Knowingly contributing malware, destructive behavior, unsafe command execution, or code intended to compromise users or maintainers.
- Retaliating against someone who makes a good-faith report or participates in a review.

Disagreement about design, priority, licensing, or review is not by itself a conduct violation. Disagreement must still be expressed without personal attacks, threats, discrimination, or publication of private data.

### Reporting a conduct concern

For a community or behavior concern, use [GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting/Security Advisories](https://github.com/KittodGG/RepoSentinel/security/advisories/new) when available, or contact the maintainer through a private method listed in the repository settings. Do not create a public Issue when the report contains personal information, private conversation, credentials, private keys, or details that could endanger someone.

For a technical vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For a normal bug, use the [Bug Report](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/bug_report.md). For a feature or rule proposal, use the [Feature Proposal](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/feature_proposal.md).

### Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports proportionally to the risk, preserve reporter confidentiality where reasonably possible, and request only safe additional evidence. Possible actions include clarification, a warning, content removal, a temporary participation restriction, or removal from the project. Decisions will consider the available facts, impact, pattern of behavior, and community safety.

Maintainers may remove or redact public content that exposes secrets, private data, unpatched vulnerabilities, or targeted harassment. Emergency action may be taken to protect users before a full review is complete.

### Scope and limitations

This Code of Conduct does not promise a response time, a particular outcome, or absolute protection of a reporter’s identity. Do not send unnecessary personal or confidential information. By participating in RepoSentinel, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and help keep the project safe, respectful, and productive.

## Bahasa Indonesia

RepoSentinel berkomitmen membangun komunitas yang welcoming, respectful, constructive, dan aman. Code of Conduct ini berlaku di repository, Issue, Pull Request, code review, Discussion, ruang project resmi, event project, serta komunikasi maintainer dengan contributor.

### Perilaku yang diharapkan

Peserta diharapkan berkomunikasi secara profesional, berasumsi baik saat meminta klarifikasi, mengkritik ide dan implementasi—bukan pribadi—menghormati perspektif yang berbeda, menerima koreksi secara terbuka, menjaga informasi privat, dan mempertahankan diskusi agar tetap relevan dengan masalah teknis atau dokumentasi yang sedang dibahas.

Saat melaporkan masalah, berikan hanya konteks minimum yang aman. Gunakan bahasa netral, sanitasi data personal dan rahasia, serta beri maintainer kesempatan yang wajar untuk memahami dan menangani masalah tersebut.

### Perilaku yang tidak dapat diterima

Perilaku berikut tidak dapat diterima:

- Harassment, diskriminasi, ancaman, hate speech, atau serangan pribadi.
- Doxxing, kontak yang tidak diinginkan, intimidasi, stalking, atau publikasi percakapan privat tanpa persetujuan.
- Perilaku atau konten seksual yang diarahkan kepada peserta lain tanpa persetujuan yang jelas.
- Gangguan yang disengaja, impersonation, spam, atau manipulasi jahat terhadap proses project.
- Mengirim credential, private key, isi `.env`, proprietary source code, atau detail vulnerability yang belum ditambal melalui channel publik.
- Dengan sengaja menyisipkan malware, perilaku destruktif, unsafe command execution, atau code yang dimaksudkan untuk membahayakan pengguna atau maintainer.
- Retaliasi terhadap orang yang membuat laporan dengan itikad baik atau ikut dalam review.

Perbedaan pendapat mengenai design, prioritas, lisensi, atau review tidak dengan sendirinya merupakan pelanggaran. Perbedaan tersebut tetap harus disampaikan tanpa serangan pribadi, ancaman, diskriminasi, atau publikasi data privat.

### Pelaporan masalah perilaku

Untuk masalah perilaku atau komunitas, gunakan [GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting/Security Advisories](https://github.com/KittodGG/RepoSentinel/security/advisories/new) bila tersedia, atau hubungi maintainer melalui metode privat yang tercantum pada repository settings. Jangan membuat Issue publik jika laporan berisi informasi personal, percakapan privat, credential, private key, atau detail yang dapat membahayakan seseorang.

Untuk technical vulnerability, ikuti [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Untuk bug biasa, gunakan [Bug Report](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/bug_report.md). Untuk proposal feature atau rule, gunakan [Feature Proposal](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/feature_proposal.md).

### Penanganan

Maintainer akan meninjau laporan sesuai risikonya, menjaga kerahasiaan reporter sejauh memungkinkan, dan hanya meminta bukti tambahan yang aman. Tindakan dapat berupa klarifikasi, peringatan, penghapusan konten, pembatasan partisipasi sementara, atau penghentian partisipasi pada project. Keputusan mempertimbangkan fakta yang tersedia, dampak, pola perilaku, dan keselamatan komunitas.

Maintainer dapat menghapus atau menyamarkan konten publik yang mengekspos secret, data privat, vulnerability yang belum ditambal, atau harassment yang ditargetkan. Tindakan darurat dapat dilakukan untuk melindungi pengguna sebelum review lengkap selesai.

### Batasan

Code of Conduct ini tidak menjanjikan response time, hasil tertentu, atau perlindungan absolut terhadap identitas reporter. Jangan mengirim data personal atau rahasia yang tidak diperlukan. Dengan berpartisipasi di RepoSentinel, Anda menyetujui aturan ini dan membantu menjaga project tetap aman, saling menghormati, dan produktif.
